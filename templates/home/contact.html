Rental Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about renting a car or bike with WheelX in Morbi.

What documents are required to rent a vehicle?

You need a valid Driving License (DL) and a government-issued photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card or Passport). Document verification takes less than 5 minutes at pickup.

Is security deposit required?

We maintain minimal, fully refundable security deposits for all self-drive cars and bikes, refunded immediately upon vehicle return.

Are there kilometre limits on daily rentals?

Our standard daily rental packages come with generous daily kilometre allowances, with optional unlimited km upgrades available upon request.

Can I book a vehicle for outstation travel from Morbi?

Yes! All WheelX vehicles carry valid Gujarat state permits and comprehensive insurance, making them ready for intercity trips to Rajkot, Ahmedabad, Dwarka, Somnath, and beyond.
